Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Our team is happy to help.

How much does a full home interior cost?

A 2 BHK typically ranges from ₹4–8 lakhs and a 3 BHK from ₹6–12 lakhs, depending on scope and finishes. Try the cost estimator above for an instant ballpark.

How long will my project take?

Modular work is delivered in 45 days from design sign-off. Full renovations with civil work usually take 60–90 days. Your timeline is fixed in the contract.

Do you charge for the first consultation?

No. Your first meeting, site measurement and initial estimate are completely free with no obligation.

What warranty do you offer?

We offer a 10-year warranty on modular furniture and a 1-year warranty on services, plus free post-handover care visits.

Can I see the design before work starts?

Absolutely. You get photo-real 3D renders and a VR walkthrough of every room, with unlimited revisions before you approve.

Do you offer EMI options?

Yes. Our banking partners offer EMIs from 6 to 36 months, including 0% interest plans on select tenures.
